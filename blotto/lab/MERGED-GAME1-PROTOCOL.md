**Merged Game 1 study, version 1**

All previous human/machine scenarios are known development evidence. Preserve the
pre-human baseline, original studies, and human challenge unchanged. This study
merges their components, selects new fixed allocations, freezes them, and then
constructs new generator and interacting-learner challenges. It does not estimate
applicant proportions, choose final submissions, or start Game 2.

Development includes 121 original machine scenarios, 48 exact human-coalition
variants, 16 broad-scout sampled variants, and 32 final/time-average human-learning
populations: 217 individual scenarios. Keep static behavior and strategic/learning
constructions separately labeled. The equilibrium control remains a diagnostic.

Five declared reference components are: the original five-group-balanced behavior
reference; an equal average of exact-open, exact-heavy, broad-open, broad-heavy
human references; old strategic generations/archives balanced by their three source
groups; point-learned populations; and win-learned populations. Balance paths and
final/average snapshots explicitly. The two favorite coalitions are not given extra
weight in this main reference. Family counts do not infer population shares.

Declare a mixture P(h,l,w,z): z*old_strategic + (1-z)*[(1-l)*((1-h)*machine_behavior
+h*human_static)+l*((1-w)*point_learners+w*win_learners)]. Evaluate all corners of
h in [0,1], l in [0,0.5], w in [0,1], z in [0,0.25]. These caps are hypotheses, not
measurements. The map is multi-affine; every intermediate population is a convex
combination of corners. Worst score and worst regret on the full parameter box
therefore attain their worst cases at corners. Preserve duplicate corner aliases,
without converting their count into expected-population weight.

Search the full 4,598,126-action space within every exact pure-security class for
score and nominal minimax regret on: original machine scenarios, human-static
scenarios, all static behavior, learned populations, merged scenarios, the five
reference means, and the corner-prior uncertainty set. Also optimize the midpoint
prior and individual corners as expected-score comparisons. Save exact conditional
optima, tie counts, and security-floor results. Distinguish a security floor from
adversarial-contamination regret, whose benchmark would change.

Predeclare eight primary selections: merged max-min; merged minimax regret;
merged minimax regret with security at least two and at least three; midpoint-prior
expected-score maximum; midpoint score with security at least two; static-behavior
minimax regret with security at least two; corner-prior minimax regret with security
at least two. Retain previous machine and literal human vectors, range fits, and
all new conditional-optimum witnesses as exploratory candidates. Save and hash the
whole set and all selection inputs before generating evaluation populations.

Validation generates 20 fresh populations of 200,000 vectors: each minimal priority
coalition under geometric scouts/value-multinomial allocation, round-number scouts/
uniform allocation, and uniform scouts/noisy attention; plus five four-field
priority variants. Audit generates 15 further populations: a fifth-field threshold
of 52 rather than 50, inverse value attention, and correlated outside budgets.
All laws and seeds are saved before selection. These are declared hypothesis
challenges, not samples of actual applicants. Report all frozen candidates.

Also run 16 new interacting-learner paths after the freeze. Each path has point
and win-credit types at fixed shares, learning against their combined population.
Payoff units cannot change the type shares. Compare replicator and payoff-replacement
updates, declared win-type shares, mutation settings, and new starting conditions.
Start from the prior 331-vector human/machine pool, without adding the newly selected
candidate vectors. Every 100 steps, admit a legal full-space official-point best
response to the current quantized population, seed it in both types, and retain all
old actions. Win-type optimization remains restricted to available actions.

Save and certify final/time-average mixtures for validation and audit. The types'
updates are approximate numerical behavioral constructions; the saved-mixture
point security certificates are exact rational computations. Never infer Nash
convergence or applicant frequencies from a fixed iteration count. Mutation and
innovation prevent a missing counter from being excluded forever.

Verify all conditional global selections through an independent integer-upper-bound
full-space scan. Check new generators, the multi-affine corner identity, fixed type
shares, deterministic interrupted-path recovery, support/mass accounting, all score
cells, pure counters, input/source hashes, and the freeze boundary. Direct sampled
scenario bounds use simultaneous DKW inequalities conditional on the fixed generator
laws. They do not cover model validity or the induced learning populations. Any
redesign based on challenge results needs another subsequent fresh challenge.
