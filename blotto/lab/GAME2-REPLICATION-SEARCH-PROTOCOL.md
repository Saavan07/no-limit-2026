Transplant the original influential field weights onto each rebuilt catalog as a control that isolates within-family sampling. The sampled payoff polynomial uses 64 own draws per family and 32 trios for every unordered family triple. Before new numerical validation, freeze those fields together with the previous 87 accepted hypotheses and all 17 originally supplied fields. Apply two independent validation passes with 262,144 new opponent trios each. There is still opponent-sampling error and uncertainty in the sampled catalog's relationship to human behavior. Tolerances remain diagnostics, not identification or posterior probabilities.

Record the original two castle-pair candidates' scores for mechanism checks, without using those scores in fitting, retention, or opponent weighting. Report independently rebuilt anchors and refits separately. Repeated models compatible with the same public scores do not constitute independent observations of an applicant population.

**Exact scoring of every legal allocation on a sampled field**

For a fixed opponent trio, its five maxima and winner-identity masks determine the focal ranking payout for all 3^5 comparisons (lose, tie, win at each castle). In quarter-payout units this is an integer table. Take finite differences along each comparison axis. The focal payoff becomes a sum of signed orthant indicators, with lower corners at zero, each maximum, or one above it. Duplicate zero-threshold corners correctly combine when all opponents bid zero.

Accumulate the signed integer counts at those corners on the downward-closed integer simplex whose coordinate sum is at most 100. A corner whose sum exceeds 100 cannot affect any legal allocation. Five inclusive prefix passes recover the exact sample payout sums. The array has 96,560,646 signed 64-bit entries; extracting its sum-100 boundary yields every one of the 4,598,126 legal submissions. Opponent identities and joint allocations remain intact throughout. This is not a castle-marginal or pairwise approximation.

The method costs a bounded number of corner updates per sampled trio and five passes over the simplex, instead of scoring every action against every trio separately. The unmodified supplied lookup source defines prizes and ties. New tests compare exhaustive small-game profiles, full-budget packing and inverse indices, serial/parallel lookup sums, and actual full-budget populations with the independent foundation scorer. Source/library hashes are preserved; generated native binaries are rebuildable and excluded from releases.

**Full-space discovery and selection**

Include fields qualified by both new CDF passes, the old 87-field set, and original supplied reference fields. Freeze all field roles before new entrant draws. Each field is an alternative hypothesis, not a probability-weighted scenario.

Run complete-action discovery on 65,536 fresh trios per field, then a separate complete-action selection on 524,288 new trios per field. Save integer score sums for the entire action universe. Reconstruct maximin and minimax-regret objectives from those scores. Report the qualified set as the primary rule and four predefined alternatives: the old 87, their union with qualified fields, the original 17, and qualified fields excluding the equal-centered catalogs. Exact maximin ties choose the lexicographically smallest allocation.

Freeze a small final comparison panel from those complete-space selections and retain all historical baselines. The primary candidate is selected before confirmation. No entry is admitted or substituted using confirmation outcomes.

**Fresh confirmation and what the bounds mean**

Evaluate the frozen confirmation panel on 1,048,576 independent trios per field. Save quarter-point sums and all pair cross-products. Use half the numerical alpha for a two-sided Hoeffding union over every legal action and evaluated field in selection. Use the other half for empirical Bernstein mean and unordered-pair bounds over the frozen confirmation panel. Conditional on the field laws, these jointly bound the selected candidate's shortfall relative to the best legal fixed allocation for the primary minimum-across-models objective. They exclude population-model error and do not establish optimality against actual hidden submissions.

The complete-action selection is an empirical selection phase with uniform finite-class error control. Confirmation uses new opponent draws after freezing the candidate panel. The fitting, validation, discovery, selection and confirmation seeds are disjoint and recorded. Rebuilt-model repeatability and numerical optimality are reported separately from real-population uncertainty.
