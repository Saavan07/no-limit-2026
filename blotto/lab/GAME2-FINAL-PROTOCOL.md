**Game 2 frozen final challenge and recommendation, version 1**

Preserve all earlier studies, sources, human vectors, and checkpoints. Treat all
previous results, including analytic counter-populations, as known development
evidence. The purpose is to choose a practical fixed Game 2 recommendation from a
frozen field and show its uncertainty. Game 1 stays (3,8,4,44,41). Nothing is submitted.

Before any new challenge draws or arena training, build a development field from
the previous 3,480 allocations, a broad high-castle-4 grid, small transfers around
the previous thirteen primaries, and 256 unrestricted seeded allocations. Score
only new actions on the saved 56 development cells. Preserve the original
conditional-cell order. Freeze previous 78 precision candidates, top 16 combined
and reference minima, top 8 under each family-copy cap, 24 best high-four vectors,
small transfers around the two latest contenders, five pure forts, and 24 seeded
unfitted controls. Deduplicate. No action admitted by the new arenas may enter the
frozen recommendation field unless it was already frozen. All training and
challenge definitions, source hashes, seeds, and selection policy are fixed first.

Use four new residual styles: coalitions, rounded budgets, sparse edges, and
counter-aware priorities. Cross four residual family mixtures (balanced,
low-heavy, intermediate-heavy, and near-heavy). Each law combines low fifth bids,
intermediate fifth bids, and near-all-in fifth bids with new first-four priorities,
concentrations, rounding, cheap bids, pure-fort atoms, and local threshold changes.
Use full legal vectors. Include exact castle-4 all-ins so castle 4 is not protected
by an artificial upper cap. Condition each law on zero through three exact all-in
five copies. These 16 references share the deterministic three-copy cell. Use
32,768 shared triples per cell. Report full copy curves. Primary core scores
average equally over the 16 residual laws and p=0,0.10,0.25,0.50. The four p values
are coverage conventions, not inferred prevalence.

Add twelve pressure scenarios: threshold-heavy castle-4 populations, concentrated
fort-4 populations with clone crowding, cheap-overbid counter clouds, and shifted
near-all-in populations. Mix these with broad backgrounds at 10%, 30%, and 60%.
Mix full-vector populations at the person level before drawing groups. These are
a declared stress block, not the predicted population. Separately score every
frozen candidate against all known pure counter-populations from the previous
probe and the five pure forts. Exact homogeneous stress results do not enter the
primary three-block selection objective.

Run eight new official-rank arenas: replicator versus prior-weighted score
replacement, fifth-bid versus shape-balanced prior, and balanced versus heavy-five
initial shares. The fifth prior gives equal mass to four fifth-bid classes. The
shape prior gives 20% to the five pure forts, 30% to other fifth bids 0-3, 30% to
fifth bids 4-79, and 20% to fifth bids 80-99, uniform within unique actions. These
are two explicit modeling assumptions. Both divide action mass among duplicate
labels, and retain every action. Use 0.5% prior-weighted mutation and 25% historical
opponents. Start with the frozen field plus known counters, previous seeds, and
48 random controls. Run 400 steps with 1,024 fresh fitness triples per step.
Every 50 steps through step 300, search current/history responses on 2,048 triples,
retain archive proposals, threshold and large mutations, exact fixed-group
responses, and inverse pure-population counters. Independently gate selected
rank responses against incumbents on 8,192 new triples, with a 96-gate simultaneous
paired bound. Admissions follow their source rules regardless of gate resolution.
The last 100 steps admit no actions; mutation and learning continue. Preserve RNG
state and checked interrupted-run recovery. No convergence or global best-response
claim follows from these dynamics.

Evaluate frozen candidates on independent shared draws from final, whole-run,
and last-100 arena populations, 32,768 triples each. For the primary arena block,
each path has equal weight and its whole-run and tail means receive equal weight.
Final snapshots are diagnostic only. Fresh draws from trained arenas test score
estimation, not unseen behavioral laws. The eight paths are model comparisons,
not a sample of real applicants.

Predeclare the primary recommendation rule: for each of three blocks (new core,
pressure scenarios, adapting arenas), compute each candidate's mean official
payout. Within each block, the comparator is the best mean in the frozen field.
Minimize the largest block regret; exact ties maximize the equal-block mean and
then choose the lexicographically smallest allocation. Also report equal-block
mean, lowest individual-model score, and low-copy core alternatives. These are
sensitivity comparisons, not replacements for the primary rule after seeing the
results. Population blocks have equal priority as coverage categories; their
counts and weights are not applicant-frequency estimates. Selection among frozen
candidates is allowed, with simultaneous sampling bounds reported. No fitting of
new candidates or generators to final outcomes is allowed in this version.

Verify the entire development screen, exact selection accounting, freeze before
all new training/draws, all legal challenge draws, the optimized scorer against
the foundation scorer for every frozen comparison, all pure-population stresses,
complete arena replay and gates, prior masses and duplicate invariance, source
and artifact integrity, and unchanged earlier checkpoints. Save a versioned Game 2
recommendation with alternatives, population shares, discovered response gaps,
and limitations. A recommendation is a decision under the stated coverage policy,
not proof of a global optimum, Nash equilibrium, or competition success.
