**Game 2 population-weight and failure review, version 1**

All earlier studies and checkpoints remain immutable. This stage uses their outcomes
as development evidence. It does not forecast applicants, select a final Game 2
submission, or claim new behavioral-model holdouts. Game 1 stays (3,8,4,44,41).

Represent opponent populations as P = p*A + (1-p)*Q, with A=(0,0,0,0,100).
For fixed Q, official expected payout is a cubic in p, obtained by conditioning on
zero, one, two, or three other exact copies. Always retain full legal joint bids.
Use three additional residual families: fifth bids 0-3, 4-79, and 80-99. Their
internal laws combine broad first-four draws, priority profiles, and exact known
vectors. Fit all twenty unordered opponent-family count cells for A/L/M/H. This
multinomial degree-three tensor evaluates mixtures correctly; linear interpolation
of whole-population scores would not. Report full p=0..1 sensitivity and four caps
0.10/0.25/0.50/1.00. These are hypotheses, not plausible-frequency estimates.

Also retain twelve distinct non-copy references: current versus whole-history
pools for each learning rule and rank-only versus mixed objective group (eight),
and the four static challenge families (four). Pool component paths equally within
each named reference, remove exact copies and renormalize, and report the removed
copy mass. Do not merge current with history or the learning rules. Static
references use fresh draws from their known generator laws, conditioned on not A.

Build a finite candidate field from all 2,867 previous candidates, local transfers
around frozen primaries, exact clone counters, and 256 unrestricted seeded vectors.
Use 2,048 development triples per conditional cell. On the 66-point 10% residual
family grid, optimize p continuously within each cap using the empirical cubics.
Select six rules: family-grid worst score for four caps, reference worst score for
cap 0.25, and the joint family/reference worst score for cap 0.25. These are finite
candidate searches, with residual-composition grid screening, not global action
optima. Freeze these choices and a shortlist including old comparisons before a
separate precision bank of 32,768 triples per cell. New draws check estimation
under the stated laws, not generalization to unseen models.

For frozen primary candidates, bound the minimum empirical score over the entire
continuous family simplex with p below each cap. The cubic is a symmetric tensor.
On a simplex region, the tensor contracted against all triples of its vertices
provides Bernstein coefficients whose minimum bounds the polynomial below.
Subdivide regions along an edge, retaining a complete partition, until the bound
gap is at most 0.015 or the declared region limit. Save the partition and a legal
upper witness. An independent rational audit checks every leaf, its coverage, and
the witness. Report empirical optimization tolerance separately from simultaneous
sampling error. A simultaneous bound on the conditional cell means extends to all
mixture weights by convexity. It does not validate family laws or constrain the
unknown real population to these families.

Review failure mechanisms: castle-win/split rates, castle points, first-place
credit, per-placement credit, fifth-bid thresholds, exact clone counters, and
sensitivity to all-in prevalence. Save all primary/reference copy coefficients
and score curves. Use signed paired differences only where their sampling basis
is clear; near-equal rankings are unresolved.

Isolate representation effects on fixed support. With the original 121-vector pool,
run 160 score-replacement steps using 1,024 fresh shared triples per step, common
initial shares and seed, and 0.5% mutation. Artificially assign ten identical labels
to either A, low-five entries, or intermediate-five entries, or use one label per
entry. Flat-label replacement is proportional to multiplicity times fitness.
A family-balanced prior assigns equal mass to the four fifth-bid classes and equal
mass to unique actions within class, dividing an action's mass among duplicate
labels. Run all eight cases and check exact trajectory invariance in the balanced
cases. This is a controlled fixed-support diagnostic, not a new evolving arena or
proof that family balance describes humans. Audit complete deterministic replay
and direct scoring. Keep the representation schemes separate from weight models.

Verify laws, conditional sampling and score sums, exact small-support polynomial
identities, continuous cubic extrema, full-precision primary scores through the
foundation scorer, all robust partitions, freeze/source hashes, representation
replay, and unchanged earlier checkpoints. Preserve no-final-selection status.
The following stage remains a candidate freeze and a fresh final challenge after
reviewing this development evidence.
