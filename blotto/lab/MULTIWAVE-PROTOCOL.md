**Game 1 multiwave study, version 1**

Save this protocol before running the study. The preceding study's entire set of
27 scenarios, including its former holdouts, is now known development evidence.
Its 23 frozen candidates remain baseline challengers. Preserve those runs unchanged.
The teammates' private strategy ideas are reserved for a later stage.

Add eight unfitted development families: value-favored subsets, cheap scouts with
three contested fields, a fifth-castle fortress, skipping one field, round tens,
one-above-anchor attacks, heterogeneous value sensitivity, and monotone ladders.
Exact definitions and seeds are code/configuration inputs. None has an estimated
frequency among applicants. Save legal joint draws and exact marginal tables.

Construct five declared group references: analytic controls, historical transfers,
anchor perturbations, support/rounding patterns, and the eight new families.
Average within each group as a search convention, not an inferred prior. The three
old strategic constructions are separate development challenges. The certified
near-equilibrium mixture is a diagnostic control excluded from candidate objectives.

Run two independent development replicates of two memory policies for eight waves.
Both policies start with the same legal seed support: old frozen candidates plus
all development-model best responses. Each wave generates 192 exact responses to
varied beliefs about group references and the policy's current strategic memory.
Use coupled belief seeds across the two policies. Latest-only remembers the most
recent generation; retain-history remembers seed mass and every prior generation.
Keep a complete shadow archive even in the latest-only experiment, so no result or
counter is lost. Each model is exact for its saved support and integer masses.

Choose uniformly among exact best-response ties using DP counts. Add legal random
mutants independently with probability 0.5, two transfers of up to eight soldiers.
Retain these mutants without claiming they are optimal or sampled human behaviors.
Record the optimal response, its belief, exact target score, tie count, and mutation.
Recompute full-space security and exploitability for generations and archives.
Iteration count, a scalar champion, and within-pool average score are not measures
of universal progress. Pairwise constant sum fixes the within-pool mean at 7.5.

Four global pure-vector objectives cover all 4,598,126 allocations: max-min score
and minimax regret on behavior models, and on behavior plus old strategic models,
every development generation, and each final shadow archive. Lexicographic tie
selection and exact optimum counts apply. Also retain each development specialist,
all old candidates, and sensitivity winners for solver shares 0,25,50,75,100 percent.
Freeze this entire shortlist and hashes of all selection inputs before evaluation.

Validation constructs fresh samples of all eight new development families, three
withheld families (inverse value preference, two-field commitment, and a three-unit
grid), and a new pair of eight-wave arenas whose references include the withheld
families. Final audit constructs three other families (rotating templates, budget
blocks, and even thresholds), then two eight-wave arenas with a declared regime
shift after wave four, from development references to the new audit families.
Evaluation seed supports use the same pre-freeze support, not new candidate vectors.
These arena scenarios depend on their stated environments, not on attacking a
candidate after observing its evaluation. Pure targeted counters are reported
separately. Report every frozen candidate and every evaluation scenario. Do not
retune this study after seeing any evaluation result.

All sample-based scores are exact conditional on saved draws. Simultaneous DKW
bounds describe direct sampled-generator scores, conditional on generator definitions
and fitted historical parameters. They do not validate the models, historical
transfers, belief weights, or induced adaptive response populations. An adaptive
support is an exact computational construction, not an IID estimate of applicants.
A mixture certificate does not protect a particular fixed vector drawn from it.

Verify selection with an independent complete-action integer-upper-bound audit.
Check generator legality, tied-response sampling, memory accounting, deterministic
checkpoint/resume, source hashes, score tables, exact counters, and candidate freeze.
Save source snapshots, all exact supports, beliefs, mutations, samples, chronology,
and progress. Neither final submissions nor Game 2 are part of this study.
