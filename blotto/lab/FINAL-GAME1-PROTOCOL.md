**Final Game 1 frozen challenge, version 1**

All prior reports, including the failure atlas and its former validation/audit
outcomes, are known development evidence. Keep the two earlier checkpoints and
all prior sources unchanged. This stage supplies fresh constructed challenges;
it does not measure applicant frequencies or choose the final application vector.
Game 2 and the human four-player ideas remain reserved.

Selection uses only the merged midpoint reference P0 and the ten failure cases
selected by the previous diagnostic. The original eight merged primary rules and
previous machine max-min remain primary comparisons. Add three predeclared rules,
all requiring exact pure security at least two:

- Minimax regret for P=(1-alpha)*P0+alpha*Q, alpha in [0,0.10].
- The same rule with alpha in [0,0.25].
- Expected score for 90% P0 plus 10% of a failure reference that gives equal weight
  to evidence kinds, then equal weight to scenarios within each kind.

Q may be any convex mixture of the ten known cases. The complete uncertainty set
is the convex hull of P0 and the ten cap-replacement populations. Score is linear
and regret is convex in opponent populations, so its maximum is attained at a
vertex. The cap is replacement mass, not total prevalence of a type. The balanced
failure mean is an explicit comparison convention, not an inferred population.
Search every legal allocation within each exact security class for these three
rules. Use all exact ties and a lexicographic primary. Preserve all old 313 vectors,
all previous diagnostic interval/boundary vectors, and new conditional witnesses
as exploratory candidates. Freeze every candidate, role, primary, input table,
source hash, configuration, and challenge definition before generating challenge
rows or running challenge arenas. Ordinary small test fixtures and a separate
arena seed-pool construction are not evaluation data.

Generate two prespecified phases, challenge and confirmation, without selecting
or replacing candidates between them. Each has 26 populations of 200,000 legal
joint vectors. For each of the five minimal winning coalitions, test four laws:
soft priorities with broader scouts and noisy target shares; round-number heaping
plus small legal transfers; substantial bids in the middle at castle 5; and a
contrarian mixture that sometimes prioritizes the coalition complement. Add five
four-field priority laws and an all-field diffuse law. Confirmation changes noise,
scout ranges, heaping units, middle-five support, and priority concentration. Exact
parameters, definitions, and seeds are in configuration and frozen source.

Construct six declared static mixtures per phase from these new components:
balanced; favorite coalitions 35/134; five-priority; five-avoiding; round-number;
and middle-five emphasis. These mixtures are conditional sensitivity hypotheses.
They reuse component tables, not independent samples. Also combine the balanced
static reference with fresh final/average learner means at shares 0.10,0.25,0.50.

Run twelve new arenas, six per phase, for 1,200 steps each. Cross point/win type
shares 0,0.5,1 with two update rules. Challenge uses 1% mutation and a dense start;
confirmation uses zero mutation and a sparse start. This is a stress design, not
an isolated causal comparison of mutation. Type proportions stay fixed; point
and win payoff units cannot alter them. Start from the previous 331-vector human
pool plus 128 separately seeded legal vectors, without deliberately inserting
newly selected finalists. Retain every strategy. Every 100 steps admit exact
point responses to both the current and time-average population, plus two legal
large-transfer mutations. Seed new actions equally in both types. Win learning
remains restricted to the retained finite pool. Retain final and time-average
legal quantized populations and exact official-point exploitability certificates.
No iteration count is interpreted as equilibrium convergence.

Three declared comparison summaries per phase are: mean score across eight equally
weighted family means (the four coalition-law families, four-field, diffuse,
arena final, arena average); minimum score across those family means; and largest
regret/minimum score across all individual static and arena snapshot scenarios.
The mixture diagnostics are reported separately and excluded from these summaries
to avoid counting reused components again. Report all frozen candidates and every
primary. Means and scenario counts do not estimate actual population frequencies.
No final winner is selected by a post-hoc combination of these metrics.

For direct sampled populations, report a simultaneous 95% DKW score-error bound
across all 52 generators, conditional on their laws. Convex derived mixtures inherit
the maximum component error. This bound does not validate the laws or cover the
learning dynamics. Use exact rational scores and regrets for saved distributions.
Check fresh sample regeneration, legal joint rows, derived weights, checkpoint and
freeze integrity, fixed type shares, replay, innovation targets/mutations, and all
score cells. Independently verify the three selections by a complete-action integer
upper-bound scan, with independent outcome-pattern security. Compare novel helpers
against brute-force small-game fixtures. Preserve failed attempts if correction is
needed and document whether any evaluation evidence existed before the correction.

The selection review after this challenge must make the population and risk
tradeoffs explicit. Add another research stage only for a material flaw; ordinary
tradeoffs among valid candidates are selection evidence, not an endless-search
requirement. A fresh challenge is new evidence only until it informs redesign.
